# Crocs-DCF-Valuation
Equity valuation of Crocs Inc. using a discounted cash flow model, financial forecasts and relative valuation analysis.
## Project Overview
Independent equity research and valuation of Crocs Inc. (NASDAQ: CROX) using a discounted cash flow (DCF) model and relative valuation analysis.

## Valuation Methodology
- Revenue and free cash flow forecasting
- Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)
- Terminal value and implied share price
- Relative valuation using P/E and EV/EBITDA
- WACC and terminal growth sensitivity analysis

## Key Investment Drivers
- International revenue growth
- North American consumer demand
- Operating margins and free cash flow generation
- Competitive valuation relative to peers
